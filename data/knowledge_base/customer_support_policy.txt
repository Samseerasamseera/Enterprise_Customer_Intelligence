CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Purpose:
This document describes the recommended approach for handling customer support issues and identifying customers who may require additional attention.

Support Ticket Guidelines:

Customers with no or very few support tickets generally require standard support handling.

Customers with multiple support tickets should be reviewed for repeated or unresolved problems.

Customers with a high number of support tickets may require proactive follow-up.

Recommended Support Actions:

1. Review the customer's previous support interactions.
2. Identify repeated complaints or unresolved issues.
3. Determine whether the issue is related to product usability, service quality, billing, or technical problems.
4. Escalate unresolved critical issues to the appropriate support team.
5. Follow up with the customer after resolution.

High-Priority Support Customers:

Customers should receive higher support priority when they have:
- Multiple unresolved tickets.
- Repeated complaints.
- High customer value.
- High predicted churn risk.

Support and Retention:

Support activity can be an important signal when evaluating customer churn risk. However, a high number of support tickets does not automatically mean that a customer will churn.

Customer support information should therefore be combined with other customer behavior and machine learning signals before making retention decisions.
