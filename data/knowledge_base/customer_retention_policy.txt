CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Purpose:
This document defines the standard customer retention actions for customers identified as having a high probability of churn.

Customer Risk Levels:

LOW RISK:
Customers with churn probability below 45% are classified as low risk.

Recommended Actions:
- Continue normal customer engagement.
- Provide regular product communication.
- Monitor customer activity.
- No immediate retention discount is required.

MEDIUM RISK:
Customers with churn probability between 45% and 70% are classified as medium risk.

Recommended Actions:
- Contact the customer proactively.
- Review recent support interactions.
- Offer personalized assistance.
- Provide relevant product recommendations.
- Consider a limited retention offer when appropriate.

HIGH RISK:
Customers with churn probability of 70% or higher are classified as high risk.

Recommended Actions:
- Prioritize the customer for immediate intervention.
- Contact the customer through the preferred communication channel.
- Review unresolved support issues.
- Assign the customer to a retention specialist when required.
- Consider a targeted retention incentive.
- Monitor the customer's activity after intervention.

Retention Priorities:

Priority should be given to customers who have:
- High churn probability.
- High customer lifetime value.
- Multiple recent support issues.
- Significant purchase history.

Business Rule:
Retention actions should consider both churn risk and customer value. A high-risk customer with high lifetime value should receive higher priority than a low-value customer with similar churn risk.

Important:
Churn probability represents a model prediction and should not be treated as a guaranteed outcome.
